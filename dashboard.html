Disease Risk Factors and Mitigations

Here are some common disease risk factors and mitigation strategies:

  • High BMI: Increase physical activity and maintain a balanced diet.
  • High Stress Levels: Practice mindfulness and relaxation techniques.
  • Low Sleep Duration: Aim for 7-9 hours of sleep per night.
  • Smoking: Seek support to quit smoking.
